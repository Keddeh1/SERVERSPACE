What is recorded

Your name, email, optional organisation, selected topic, message, request identifier and consent version are recorded in a private durable store. They are not committed to public GitHub repositories or included in public package readbacks.

Purpose and control

The record supports owner review of your enquiry. No newsletter opt-in is inferred, and this candidate service sends no automated email. To ask about a prior enquiry, use the contact form and include its registration reference.

Retention and deployment scope

The service removes enquiry records older than 90 days through its maintenance cycle. Its cloud storage boundary is the owner’s private runtime root; this page does not claim independent legal certification or a completed public-host deployment.
