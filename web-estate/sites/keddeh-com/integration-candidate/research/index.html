0.297 software oscillator

The supplied Kuramoto model uses 0.297 as its angular-frequency reference. Wrapped phase error and soft capture support flywheel and reanchoring. The deployed adapter does not inject fabricated stochastic measurement noise.

Propagation abstraction

A common software model explores directed graphs, delays and smooth activation. Distributed gossip, neural LIF and RC circuits have distinct physical assumptions; a useful variable mapping is not by itself a proof of exact substrate equivalence.

Topology and empirical work

Evaluate explicit graph inputs and reproducible test conditions. Claims about severe-noise resilience or AI-controlled spawn reductions require their original experimental datasets and methods before they can be presented as measured customer outcomes.
