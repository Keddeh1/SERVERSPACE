# Significant-result research protocol

The user's requested standard is rigorous, considerate analytical work that tests, theorises, critiques and records emergent learning in relevant sectors. A promising result triggers a deeper case study before broader interpretation.

Required case-study content:

1. State the exact claim, source definitions, contextual jurisdiction and falsifiable hypotheses.
2. Specify invariant outcomes, comparable work, authority, durability and acknowledgement requirements.
3. Preserve full methods, source hashes and raw samples. Include controls, sensitivity/ablation and relevant adversarial cases.
4. Explain the observed mechanism through a source-derived model; instrument its predictions where feasible.
5. Report uncertainty, null/counterexamples, environment and failure-scope limits. Retain exclusions with reasons determined independently of favourable outcomes.
6. Critique alternative explanations, semantic differences and what the result does not measure.
7. File new knowledge topologically: North authority/context, East formal invariants/models, South implementation/lifecycle, West methods/readbacks/critique. Link canonical records through the formal registry.
8. Record emergent learning and the next research gate without silently promoting local findings to global production, energy, safety or valuation claims.

Use source-local meanings for KEX, IL-LLM, unit origin, vectors and lineage. Distinguish a source assertion from independently executed verification. Harvard-level quality is an analytical aspiration, not an institutional endorsement. No affiliation is claimed by filing a study here.
