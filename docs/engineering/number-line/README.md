## Four-ray arithmetic

| Ray | Operation from unit 1 | k=5 | Operation retaining the inverse relation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

For the evaluated examples, k is a positive integer parameter, not an unsigned result or necessarily an ordinal distance from the center. North k=1 and south k=1 evaluate to the unit; east/west k=1 evaluate to 2 and 0. The drawing and formula should not be treated as interchangeable step numbering. Direction is part of identity. East k=4 and north k=5 both evaluate to 5 but have different operations and histories. Scalar equality does not establish coordinate or lineage equality. Preserve axis, unit/frame, parameter, operation and predecessor context when translating state.

## Zero and state are different types

West k=1 actually evaluates to zero. A positive active-address rule must not reject that valid arithmetic result or erase the underlying lineage. The basis label |0⟩ is also distinct from the Hilbert zero vector. A stochastic measurement is not generally a deterministic map to a single observed eigenvalue. These precision points do not change the software requirement to retain measurement context and avoid conflating readout with state.
