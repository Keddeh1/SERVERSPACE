# Programme vision and decision boundaries

Build reusable sector foundries on the supplied owner HTML KEX/runtime substrate.
A customer journey should progress from a business need to a template, an explicit
admin boundary, permitted storage, a chosen address and a verified release.
Small functions derive from governed attributed contracts and retain their
invariants through larger builds; new pages must reuse qualified functions.

Two address routes remain distinct: public-domain registration and an independent
owner namespace/authoritative service using VisitUsAt, TellMeAbout, WhatIs and
WhoIs patterns. Preserve the complete customer display name and define its wire
encoding, resolver discovery and certificate trust. No namespace operation is
asserted to grant exclusive trademark rights or public-root delegation.

The owner requires 100 TB physical RAM per server. Per-service VFS allocations,
32-bit windows, cache banks, nodes and mesh lineage need actual physical admission
and per-customer boundaries. TOT, transmission, failover and the supplied 0.297
model need an authoritative protocol and explicit failure envelope. Current
journal/domain proofs are attributed as local execution, not multi-host or
all-replica loss acceptance. No physical capacity is manufactured by a VFS label.

Google identity and separately consented Drive access require different grants.
Customers cannot access owner actuators. Private source, workbooks, tokens and
personal allowlists remain outside public issues and planning exports.

APEX is the existing owner design workflow, not a certification. Its primary
standards and evidence contracts should govern market hooks, claims, considerate
UX, forms and accessibility. Market demand, cryptographic superiority, uptime and
commercial readiness are not established by aspirational copy.

Planning links exact current evidence, owners, dependencies and recovery gates.
Research starts from the specified minimums after the utility gate is satisfied;
external permissions remain explicit and have usable versioned alternatives.
